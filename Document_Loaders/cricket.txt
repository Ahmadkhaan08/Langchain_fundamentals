Cricket is a bat-and-ball game that originated in England during the 16th century. It gradually became popular in different parts of the world, especially in countries that were once part of the British Empire. Today, cricket is played internationally and is governed by the International Cricket Council (ICC).

There are three major formats of international cricket: Test cricket, One Day International (ODI), and Twenty20 (T20). Test matches are played over a maximum of five days, while ODI matches consist of 50 overs per team. T20 is the shortest format, with each team playing 20 overs.

A standard cricket match is played between two teams, each consisting of eleven players. One team bats to score runs, while the other team bowls and fields to take wickets and restrict runs. A cricket pitch is 22 yards long, and each end has three wooden stumps called wickets.

The main equipment used in cricket includes a bat, ball, wickets, batting pads, gloves, helmet, and protective guards. Players also wear special shoes designed for better grip and movement. The cricket ball is usually made of leather, while the bat is traditionally made of willow wood.

International cricket is played between national teams in different tournaments and bilateral series. Major competitions include the ICC Cricket World Cup, ICC T20 World Cup, and ICC Champions Trophy. These tournaments attract millions of viewers from around the world.

Cricket is one of the most popular sports in Pakistan. The Pakistan national cricket team has achieved several important victories, including winning the 1992 Cricket World Cup and the 2009 T20 World Cup. Pakistan has produced famous players such as Imran Khan, Wasim Akram, Waqar Younis, Babar Azam, and Shaheen Afridi.
